SOLARNET INTERNET

Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

This policy explains how SolarNet uses information in its billing, customer-support, and Facebook Page Messenger services.

Information we process

SolarNet processes customer account, contact, billing, payment-reference, installation, support-ticket, and network-service information needed to provide internet service. When someone messages the official SolarNet Facebook Page, SolarNet may store the Page-scoped sender identifier, message text, attachment metadata, timestamps, staff replies, and delivery results. SolarNet does not access personal Facebook conversations that were not sent to the SolarNet Page.

How information is used

Information is used to operate customer accounts, issue invoices and reminders, record payments, provide technical and billing support, protect the service, and maintain required operational records. Messenger information is used to answer the sender, manage human handoff, prevent duplicate replies, and document support activity. Account-specific information is directed to the secure customer portal.

Sharing, retention, and security

SolarNet shares information only with service providers needed for hosting, email, SMS, payments, support, and Facebook Page messaging, or when legally required. SolarNet does not sell Messenger conversations or customer account information. Records are retained for operational, accounting, security, dispute-resolution, and legal requirements, then deleted or anonymized when no longer required. SolarNet uses role-based staff access, encrypted connections, protected credentials, and audit records.

Your choices

Customers may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable by emailing solarnet.connection@gmail.com. Some billing, payment, security, or legal records may need to be retained.
